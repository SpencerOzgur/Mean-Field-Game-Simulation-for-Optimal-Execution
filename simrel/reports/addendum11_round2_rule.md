The rational predator passes if, for the medium or the strong rational arm (each judged separately), at size 15%, on the five pilot markets other than a03 (a03 reported separately, as in the pilot):
(a) the mean predator P&L against TWAP, pooled over the five markets, is at least 0 bp of order value; and
(b) the true cost of TWAP rises by at least 1.0 bp relative to off in at least 3 of the 5 markets.
If neither arm passes, the predatory family stops: no Test R is drawn and the result is written up as a negative finding.
No threshold, market set, arm or metric may change after this file is committed.
